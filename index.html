Meet your guide

Who Is Professor Feld?

Professor Feld is a friendly field geologist who believes the best way to understand Earth is to step outside and look closely. He helps explorers notice the clues hidden in rocks, fossils, water, and landscapes.

Professor Feld says

“The rocks are trying to tell us a story. Let’s see what we can discover.”
